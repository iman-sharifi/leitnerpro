Leitner Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

White Beard Solutions ("we", "us", "our") built the Leitner Pro mobile app (package com.whitebeardsolutions.leitnerpro) as a freemium flashcard/spaced-repetition learning app. This page explains what data the app collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have — including how to delete your account and data.
By using Leitner Pro you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We do not sell your personal data.
1. Information We Collect
a) Account information (registered users). When you create an account, we collect your email address and a username, and you may optionally provide your first and last name, age, gender, job, country and city, and a profile photo. Your password is stored only as a secure one-way hash — we never store it in plain text.
b) Guest mode (no sign-up). You can use Leitner Pro as a guest without registering. In guest mode we do not collect your email, name, or other identifying profile data; we assign an anonymous guest identifier so your study progress can be saved on our server. You only need to register (providing an email) if you choose to buy paid content. All free content is usable as a guest.
c) Learning data. The boxes and flashcards you create, import, or study, and your spaced-repetition progress (card levels, review dates, statistics).
d) Purchase data. A record of content or subscriptions you have obtained (purchase history). Paid transactions, where offered, are processed through Google Play Billing; we do not receive or store your full payment-card details.
e) Device & log data. When you use the Service, and especially if an error occurs, we and our third-party providers may collect device/log data such as your IP address, device model and name, operating system version, app configuration, and the date/time of use.
2. Third-Party Services and SDKs
The app uses the following third-party services, which may collect information used to identify you or your device:
• Google Play Services — core platform services.
• Google AdMob (advertising) — serves ads in the app and may collect your advertising ID (AAID) and other device identifiers, IP address and approximate (coarse) location, and ad-interaction/usage data, in order to deliver, measure, and (where applicable) personalize ads. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. See Google's information: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543 and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
• Google Analytics for Firebase — app usage and interaction analytics, device identifiers, and approximate location, to help us understand and improve the app.
• Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports and diagnostic/device information when the app crashes.
• Firebase Cloud Messaging — a push-notification token used to deliver notifications to your device.
These providers process data under their own privacy policies, including Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
3. How We Use Your Information
To provide and operate the Service; to save and sync your learning progress across sessions; to process purchases; to display advertising (free tier); to analyze and improve the app and fix crashes; to send notifications you have enabled; and to protect against fraud and abuse.
4. Sharing of Information
We share data only with the service providers listed in Section 2 so they can perform services on our behalf, and where required by law. We do not sell your personal information.
5. Data Retention
We keep account and learning data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal account data and associated learning/progress data from our systems (see Section 6). Some diagnostic/analytics data held by third parties is retained according to their own retention schedules.
6. Account and Data Deletion
You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time:
• In the app: open Profile → Delete Account; or
• On the web: visit our account-deletion page at https://iman-sharifi.github.io/leitnerpro/delete-account.html; or
• By email: write to help.whitebeardsolutions@gmail.com from your registered email address.
When you delete your account we remove your personal account data (email, name, profile fields) and your learning progress, boxes you own, subscriptions, and referral records from our active systems. Guest sessions hold no personal identity data.
7. Security
We value your trust and strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, including hashing passwords and encrypting data in transit over HTTPS. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
8. Children's Privacy
Leitner Pro is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it. Parents or guardians who believe their child provided us personal information should contact us.
9. Links to Other Sites
The Service may contain links to other sites that we do not operate. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those third-party sites and advise you to review their privacy policies.
10. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. This policy is effective as of 2026-11-15.
11. Contact Us
If you have any questions or requests about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at
help.whitebeardsolutions@gmail.com
